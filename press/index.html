Announcement line
Pixel Forge Jam empowers young creators to explore game development, build original games, and share their work with the world.
About Pixel Forge Jam (boilerplate)

Pixel Forge Jam is a nonprofit, student-run game development community founded by Rishabh Mittal and fiscally sponsored by Hack Club, a 501(c)(3) nonprofit. Its game jams welcome beginners and experienced developers to experiment, learn, and create original games that the community can play and rate. Pixel Forge Jam aims to introduce young people to game development and inspire them to share their creativity with the world.

Mission

What we do

Pixel Forge Jam brings developers, artists, designers, and creators together for a focused week of experimentation, learning, and collaboration, helping participants turn bold ideas into finished, playable games.

Vision

Where we are going

We envision an open and welcoming global game-making community where creators of every experience level feel empowered to take creative risks, develop their skills, share their work, and keep building long after the jam ends.
