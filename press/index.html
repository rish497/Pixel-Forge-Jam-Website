Logo do's and don'ts

  • Do keep the original proportions and colours.
  • Do leave clear space around the logo so nothing crowds it.
  • Don't stretch, rotate, recolour, or add effects to the logo.
  • Don't place the dark logo on a dark background, or the light one on a light background.
