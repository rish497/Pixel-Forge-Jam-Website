Announcing Pixel Forge Jam #3

The forge opens again for a seven-day Tricolor Challenge with more than $5,000 in prizes.

By Pixel Forge Team13 August 20264 min read

Pixel Forge Jam is back. Developers, artists, designers, musicians, and first-time creators are invited to spend one focused week turning an idea into a playable game.

Pixel Forge Jam #3 runs online from September 5 to September 12, 2026. Participants can work solo or in teams of up to four and submit their games through itch.io.

The Tricolor Challenge

This edition adds one bold creative restriction: every submitted game must visibly use exactly three colours. Engine defaults count, and fades or gradients that introduce extra visible colours are not allowed.

The game theme will be revealed when the jam begins. Your entry must respond to that theme, but how you interpret it is up to you.

Jam Details

DatesSeptember 5-12, 2026
FormatOnline, hosted on itch.io
Team sizeSolo or up to four people
ChallengeUse exactly three colours
ThemeRevealed at the start
Prize poolMore than $5,000 in prizes

What You Need to Know

  • Use any game engine you like.
  • Create the majority of your work during the jam period and credit any permitted pre-made assets.
  • Generative AI content is not allowed.
  • Include a short explanation of how your game uses the theme.
  • Browser-playable submissions are recommended, with Windows or Mac builds also accepted.
  • Play other entries and leave useful, constructive feedback during voting.
Three colours. Seven days. One finished game.

How Entries Are Rated

Community ratings consider gameplay, use of the theme, visual design, and music. The queue system asks each participant to rate five randomly selected games first, helping every submission receive attention.

Ready to Build?

Whether this is your first game or your next experiment, Pixel Forge Jam #3 is a chance to try a strong idea, work within a memorable constraint, and ship something you can share.
