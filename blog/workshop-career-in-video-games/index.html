A Career in Video Games

A free two-hour workshop from Karma Play and Pixel Forge for anyone curious about finding their place in the games industry.

By Pixel Forge Team23 September 20264 min read

Making games is a creative practice, but building a career in games can feel much less clear. This free workshop is designed to make that path easier to see.

Join Karma Play and Pixel Forge for two focused hours on the people, skills, and decisions behind a career in video games.

What the workshop covers

We will look at the different roles that bring a game to life, how portfolios communicate what you can do, and practical ways to start building experience before you have your first professional credit.

  • How game development teams work together.
  • What studios look for in early portfolios and applications.
  • How to choose a first project that demonstrates your strengths.
  • Where to find communities, mentors, and opportunities to keep learning.

Workshop details

FormatFree online workshop
LengthTwo hours
Hosted byKarma Play x Pixel Forge
AudienceAnyone curious about games careers
You do not need to have everything figured out before you start making games.

Save your place

Bring your questions, your current project, or simply your curiosity. The workshop is a chance to meet other aspiring creators and leave with a clearer next step.
